# Consult Anytime - AI Chatbot with Speech Recognition

Due to high competition within schools and the lack of emotional support, there has been a rise in depression and emotional instability among teenagers. World Health Organization (WHO) reports that 1 in 7 aged 10-19 years old experiences a mental disorder. However, some teenagers do not feel comfortable opening up to others and seek help. Our project aims to use Artificial Intelligence bot to conduct sentimental analysis based on their verbal and facial expression. The bot will serve as a listening ear and provide advice on their situation.

We implement Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) for better retrieval for mental health advisories and checks for possible ethical issues in the generated content with Speech Recognition to allow verbal interaction between the User and Chatbot.
